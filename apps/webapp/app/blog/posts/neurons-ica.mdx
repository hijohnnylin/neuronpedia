#### Token Lists

Notice in the above example that clicking certain explanations changes the top logits to different lists more relevant to that explanation or explanation method. These are "Token Lists", a new feature on Neuronpedia that which show lists of tokens associated with a neuron or latent. Token lists are directly linkable. For example, here's the link showing tokens found via the fitted Jacobian Lens for that same Qwen airplanes neuron: [https://www.neuronpedia.org/qwen3-8b/24/2665?tokenlist=jacobian-lens:J-Lens](https://www.neuronpedia.org/qwen3-8b/24/2665?tokenlist=jacobian-lens:J-Lens)

🙋 **Opportunity to Contribute**: Want to upload your labels? We have a [gated upload API](/api-doc#tag/explanations/POST/api/explanation/new) for explanations, available upon request. [Contact us](mailto:johnny@neuronpedia.org?subject=Neuron%20Explanations) and we'll set you up.

## ICA Lens

#### [Release](/icalens) | [Paper by Liu, Han et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.11722) | [Code](https://github.com/liusida/ica-lens-paper) | [Tweet](https://x.com/feijianghan/status/2112654366599504277)

ICA Lens revisits independent component analysis as a way to find interpretable directions in the residual stream, with no dictionary training needed.

Neuronpedia hosts ICA Lenses for 6 models. Each lens is for one layer, and the number of directions per lens is the model's hidden size (d_model). Each direction has its own full dashboard, like SAE latents and neurons. Using the dropdown above the logits, you can switch to viewing the tokens through J-Lens or R-Lens as well.

- [**GPT-2 Small**](/gpt2-small/icalens-res): 12 layers. Example: [US states](/gpt2-small/6-icalens-res/31).
- [**Gemma 2 2B**](/gemma-2-2b/icalens-res): 26 layers. Example: [months](/gemma-2-2b/12-icalens-res/65).
- [**Gemma 2 2B IT**](/gemma-2-2b-it/icalens-res): 26 layers. Example: [possessive pronouns](/gemma-2-2b-it/12-icalens-res/19).
- [**Qwen3.5 2B Base**](/qwen3.5-2b-pt/icalens-res): 6 layers (every 4th). Example: [first names](/qwen3.5-2b-pt/12-icalens-res/74).
- [**Qwen3.5 9B Base**](/qwen3.5-9b-pt/icalens-res): 8 layers (every 4th). Example: [music](/qwen3.5-9b-pt/16-icalens-res/352).
- [**Qwen3.6 27B**](/qwen3.6-27b/icalens-res): 16 layers (every 4th). Example: [cost and price](/qwen3.6-27b/32-icalens-res/77).

Most ICA lenses are inference enabled, too. For example, here's steering on a ["legal system" direction](https://www.neuronpedia.org/gpt2-small/8-icalens-res/51) in GPT2-Small [(steer link)](https://www.neuronpedia.org/gpt2-small/steer?saved=cmw8mpwvb001qhnlw96cvp9o5&source=8-icalens-res&index=51&strength=208.98):
